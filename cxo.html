PRODUCER PROFILE / NOT A RANKING

CXO

CXO (born Nathaniel Luciano Campos) is an American record producer and audio engineer from San Francisco, California, who has emerged as a prominent figure in the underground (UG) rap and rage/experimental trap scene.

Why CXO stands out to me

CXO does have a decently recognizable tag that tells you his instrumentals are really good. One of my favorite beats of all time is one he produced, using strong sample skills and drums that make a good impression on my ears and not so much my speakers. He's been around for quite some time in this new era but I think his production style will last.

Favorite production moments

My favorite beat and song of CXO's is "HELLRAISER" by Che, and "Love (MKB)" by Che. Big time producer for Che, but for my ears if I like the instrumental enough I can handle not-so-easy on the ear vocals very easily.

What to listen for

Unconventional Sound Effects (SFX): Listeners can often hear arcade-style laser zaps, metallic clangs, and jarring transition risers tucked into the background whenever a loop restarts.
