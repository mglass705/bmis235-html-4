Ginseng

One of five underground producers I wanted to highlight. This is a space for the production choices, tracks, and moments that make the music memorable to me.

Why Ginseng stands out to me

Ginseng (also stylized as Gin$eng) is the stage name of Christian Baello, a highly influential California-based music producer, songwriter, and multi-instrumentalist known for bridging the classic 2010s SoundCloud era with the modern underground rap scene.

Favorite production moments

Funny enough, Ginseng and a partner of his used to a pop duo called Dream, Ivory where many in my generation are familiar with their song "welcome and goodbye" from social media. But my favorite works of Ginseng's change every time he makes a new beat, but for now it's "ENJOY YOUR LIFE" by Che, and "Bbyangl" by Prettifun.

What to listen for

You can spot a Ginseng beat by listening for a mix of high-pitched, glitched-out vocal/synth textures, hyper-compressed "crushing" 808s, and specific pitched-up (FOREVER) producer tags.
