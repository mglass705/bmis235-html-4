PRODUCER PROFILE / NOT A RANKING

MISOGI

MISOGI (born Zain Siddiqui) is a London-based music producer, recording artist, and co-founder of the record label and production collective Pink Noise.

Why MISOGI stands out to me

His beats pair dreamy, shoegaze-inspired synth melodies and intricate counter-melodies with heavy distortion and aggressive trap drum programming.

Favorite production moments

His sound is best capture in songs like "NOTICE ME" by Sofaygo and Hardrock, and "First Place" by Prettifun.

What to listen for

Bitcrushed & Pitch-Shifted Textures. He frequently applies heavy bitcrushing, granular delays, or tape warble to make clean melodies sound intentionally degraded and digital.
